# SaveYourRoof Explainer Frame System

Use the established SaveYourRoof navy, cyan, green, off-white, and white palette. The composition should feel clean, measured, protective, and practical: dark navy provides trust and contrast, cyan marks process and water, green marks the optional Restore outcome, and off-white keeps the technical explanation approachable.

Use Manrope ExtraBold for step labels and the brand line, with Inter for narration captions and supporting copy. Build visuals as large, simple instructional diagrams: a roofline, controlled spray arcs, organic-growth particles breaking apart, a gentle rinse stream, and a protective oil layer. Keep the composition asymmetric enough to feel editorial, while maintaining a clear reading path from the step number to the visual action.

Every step receives a distinct accent treatment. APPLY uses cyan spray arcs. BREAK DOWN uses moss-green particles dissolving into smaller dots. RINSE uses a diagonal blue water sweep. RESTORE — THE ADD-ON uses a green protective film settling over the shingle. The final scene returns to a clean navy field with the SaveYourRoof lockup and the exact line `Clean · Restore · Save $`.

Caption treatment: white text on a translucent navy panel with a cyan rule, positioned in the lower third and sized for 16:9 readability. Avoid tiny text. Use one clear step title, one short supporting phrase, and a compact progress indicator.
